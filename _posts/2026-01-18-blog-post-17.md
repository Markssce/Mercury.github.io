# Sydney Itinerary (20 Feb - 28 Feb 2026)

**Inviter**: Jiajie Gao (UTS International Student, Student ID: 25677105, Address: Unit406, 41 Rothschild Ave, Rosebery NSW 2018)

**Invitee**: Shengguo Zheng (Tongji University Student, China)

**Purpose**: Winter vacation visit, family-friendly sightseeing, and local culture experience (accompanied by Jiajie Gao throughout the trip)

### Daily Itinerary

1. **20 Feb 2026 (Fri)**: Arrive in Sydney. Jiajie Gao picks up Shengguo Zheng from Sydney Airport. Go to the rented apartment to settle in, rest and adjust to jet lag. Dinner at a local casual restaurant nearby.
    
2. **21 Feb 2026 (Sat)**: Sydney CBD & Circular Quay. Visit Sydney Opera House (external tour + photo-taking), walk along Circular Quay, take a ferry to Mrs Macquarie's Chair for panoramic views of the Opera House and Harbour Bridge. Lunch and dinner at local cafes/restaurants.
    
3. **22 Feb 2026 (Sun)**: Sydney Harbour Bridge. Take a bridge climb tour (or pedestrian walkway for a relaxed experience). Explore The Rocks area, visit historic streets and local markets. Return to the apartment in the evening.
    
4. **23 Feb 2026 (Mon)**: Bondi Beach & Coastal Walk. Visit Bondi Beach, enjoy the coastal scenery, take a casual walk along the Bondi to Coogee Coastal Path. Try local seafood for lunch near the beach. Return to the apartment in the afternoon to rest.
    
5. **24 Feb 2026 (Tue)**: UTS Campus & Chinatown. Visit Jiajie Gao's campus (UTS) to experience the academic environment. In the afternoon, go to Chinatown for a taste of familiar food and explore the surrounding area. Dinner at a Chinese restaurant in Chinatown.
    
6. **25 Feb 2026 (Wed)**: Blue Mountains Day Trip. Take a train to Blue Mountains National Park, visit Echo Point to view the Three Sisters. Enjoy the natural scenery, take a cable car ride. Return to Sydney in the evening.
    
7. **26 Feb 2026 (Thu)**: Relaxation & Local Life. Visit a nearby supermarket and shopping mall to experience daily life in Sydney. Spend the afternoon at a local park for a casual walk. Cook dinner together at the apartment.
    
8. **27 Feb 2026 (Fri)**: Taronga Zoo. Visit Taronga Zoo to see Australian unique animals (koalas, kangaroos, etc.). Take a ferry back to Circular Quay in the afternoon. Have a farewell dinner at a characteristic local restaurant.
    
9. **28 Feb 2026 (Sat)**: Departure. Pack luggage, check out from the apartment. Jiajie Gao sends Shengguo Zheng to Sydney Airport for the return flight to China. Ensure departure before the visa expiry date.
    

### Cost & Accommodation

- **Accommodation**: Stay at Jiajie Gao's rented apartment (Unit406, 41 Rothschild Ave, Rosebery NSW 2018) during the entire trip.
    
- **Costs**: Jiajie Gao covers accommodation and local transportation; Shengguo Zheng covers international round-trip airfare and travel insurance.
    

**Note**: This itinerary is flexible and may be adjusted slightly according to weather and on-site conditions, with Jiajie Gao accompanying at all times to ensure compliance with Australian laws and visa requirements.
